Rampe

Sri Lankan and global recipes, documented properly.

What it is

Indexed by how you actually cook.

A working culinary archive of Sri Lankan home cooking alongside dishes gathered from elsewhere. Each recipe is documented with effort level, diet, heat, occasion and the story behind it.

Notion-backed and schema-first, Rampe is the public face of a private recipe database built on the same principles as The Meridian. Effort, diet, heat and occasion are fields, not tags, so the archive answers the question you actually arrive with: what can I cook tonight.
