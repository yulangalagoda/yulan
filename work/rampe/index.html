Rampe

Sri Lankan and global recipes, documented properly.

What it is

A working culinary archive of Sri Lankan home cooking alongside dishes gathered from elsewhere. Each recipe is documented with effort level, diet, heat, occasion, and the story behind it.

Notion-backed and schema-first, Rampe is the public face of a private recipe database built on the same principles as The Meridian.

How it is built

  1. 01Indexed by how you cook. Effort, diet, heat, and occasion fields on every recipe, Sri Lankan and global alike.
  2. 02Schema-first design. Every field justified and consistent across all entries.
  3. 03One source of truth. A Notion database drives the public site.
  4. 04Companion to The Meridian. The same schema-first philosophy, applied to a different collection.
  5. 05Notion-backed pipeline. Published to Cloudflare Pages at rampe.yulan.me.
