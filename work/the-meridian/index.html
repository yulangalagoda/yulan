The Meridian

A living archive of antiques, books, and historical objects.

What it is

A personal digital reliquary.

A curated, research-backed catalogue of antiques and historical items. Each entry is documented with provenance, condition, valuation notes and reference material.

The site is the public face of an extensive private Notion database, and it exists to show how a careful schema, a considered visual language and editorial writing turn a personal collection into something genuinely useful.

The visual language

Oxblood and cream, borrowed from leather-bound archives. The palette is doing work: it signals age and care before a single entry is read.
